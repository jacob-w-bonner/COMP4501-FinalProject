HOW TO RUN
The scene to run is test_scene.tscn, which is located under res://Scenes. You can open the scene then press Run Current Scene (F6) to play. You can also just press Run Project (F5) without opening the scene as well.

MOLE ACTIONS
Control the moles using LEFT CLICK / RIGHT CLICK / DOUBLE LEFT CLICK.

> All moles:
  - LEFT CLICK to select, then LEFT CLICK on another mole to attack.
  - LEFT CLICK to select, then LEFT CLICK on the terrain to burrow to that location.
> Mole:
  - DOUBLE LEFT CLICK to enter sentry mode.
> Music mole:
  - RIGHT CLICK to play music for nearby moles.
> Doctor mole:
  - RIGHT CLICK to heal nearby moles.
> Miner mole:
  - LEFT CLICK to select and LEFT CLICK on resource vein to mine.

More detailed descriptions of the mole's actions and transitions between states are included in the comments of each class script.

CAMERA CONTROLS
- WASD to pan camera
- Q/E or MOUSE ZOOM WHEEL to zoom out/in
- MIDDLE CLICK on any unit to have the camera track the unit. You can stop tracking the unit by using WASD to pan the camera around
